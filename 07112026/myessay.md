## 1. The biggest problem: you did not describe the chart, you wrote your own ideas

Task 1 asks you to **report the data**. You must not add reasons, opinions or explanations that are not in the chart. Compare:

| Your sentence | Problem |
| --- | --- |
| "world is changing so fast and no one knows everything so they need to learn for adaption" | Not in the chart. This is your own opinion (Task 2 style) |
| "Learning a new thing maybe it's just a habbit of some people" | Not in the chart. The chart says "Enjoy learning/studying: 20%" |
| "they want to challenge themself, they want to see new world or discover the new ability" | Invented. No such categories exist |
| "Most of them need to know and have a good impress about the subject" | Real category (interest in subject), but you give **no number** (40%) |
| "Some people they need more knowledge or certificate for their promotion's conditions" | Mixes two categories (qualifications 38% and promotion 20%) and gives no numbers |

**Rule:** every sentence in Task 1 should be traceable to a bar, a slice or a number.

## 2. No overview (this alone keeps you at band 5)

An overview is a short paragraph (2 sentences) with the **main trends, without numbers**. Examiners look for it. You have none. Without it, Task Achievement is capped around band 5.

Example from this chart: *Interest and qualifications were the most common reasons; meeting people and changing jobs were the least. Individuals were expected to pay the most, taxpayers the least.*

## 3. Almost no data

- Bar chart: you give **zero numbers**. The chart has 7 bars with exact values; you must use them (40%, 38%, 22%, 20%, 20%, 12%, 9%).
- Pie chart: you gave 40% and 35% (good). But you wrote "the rest" instead of 25%. Always give the figure.
- Missing categories: "helpful for current job (22%)" and "able to change jobs (12%)" are not clearly described.

## 4. Structure and organisation

Your essay: Intro (1 paragraph) → one long bar chart paragraph → short pie chart paragraph. There is no overview and no grouping.

Better: **Intro → Overview → Body 1 (bar chart) → Body 2 (pie chart)**.
In Body 1, group the bars into 3 levels instead of going through them one by one:
- top: 40%, 38%
- middle: 22%, 20%, 20%
- bottom: 12%, 9%

Your paragraph 2 is also far too long (about 165 words in one block). Keep each paragraph focused on one job.

## 5. Sentence-by-sentence feedback

**Sentence 1:** "These charts shown the adult education."
- `shown` → `show` / `illustrate` (wrong verb form).
- "the adult education" → "adult education" (no article).
- Too vague: say *what* the charts show.
- Better: *The bar chart illustrates the reasons why adults choose to study, while the pie chart shows how people think the cost of courses should be shared.*

**Sentence 2:** "The first chart shows the reason why adult keep learning and the second chart, it shows the people's thought that how costs of each costs should be shared."
- You repeated the idea from sentence 1. Do the intro in **one** sentence.
- "the reason" → "the reasons" (plural).
- "adult" → "adults".
- "the second chart, it shows" → "the second chart shows" (**do not repeat the subject with "it"**).
- "people's thought that how" → "people's opinions on how".
- "costs of each costs" → "the costs of each course" (repeated word, wrong noun).

**Sentence 3:** "In the survey, the adult opinion about the reason why they're learning new things is very abundant."
- "abundant" is the wrong word. It means "plentiful", and it cannot describe opinions. Use *varied* or *diverse*, or just delete this sentence.
- "adult opinion" → "adults' opinions".
- "they're" is a contraction: **avoid contractions in academic writing**.

**Sentence 4:** "Most of them need to know and have a good impress about the subject."
- "impress" is a verb; the noun would be "impression". The meaning is also unclear. Say *were interested in the subject*.
- "Most" is wrong: only 40% chose it, which is not "most". Use *the largest proportion* or *the most common reason*.

**Sentence 5:** "Also a lot of people they're believing that learning can help them gain more qualification in their job."
- Again the repeated subject ("a lot of people they"). **This error appears many times in your essay; fix it first.**
- "they're believing": the chart shows no beliefs, only reasons. Use *38% studied to gain qualifications*.
- "qualification" → "qualifications" (plural).
- "a lot of people" is informal and imprecise. Use the exact figure.

**Sentence 6:** "But also a lot of people, they need to improve their skill in working, world is changing so fast and no one knows everything so they need to learn for adaption."
- Run-on sentence (3 ideas joined with commas).
- "improve their skill in working" → "improve their skills at work".
- "world" → "the world".
- "adaption" → "adaptation".
- The whole second half is your opinion; delete it.
- Meaning: this probably matches "helpful for current job" (22%). Use the number.

**Sentence 7:** "Some people they need more knowledge or certificate for their promotion's conditions in their company."
- Subject repeated again ("Some people they").
- "promotion's conditions" is awkward. Use *to improve their promotion prospects* (the chart wording is "To improve prospects of promotion": 20%).
- "certificate" → "certificates" (or "qualifications").

**Sentence 8:** "Learning a new thing maybe it's just a habbit of some people."
- `maybe` → `it may be`; the sentence is broken.
- `habbit` → `habit` (spelling).
- Not in the chart. Replace with: *20% enjoyed learning or studying.*

**Sentence 9:** "Some people also said they want to challenge themself, they want to see new world or discover the new ability so that's the reason why they learn new things to."
- Invented content (see section 1).
- `themself` → `themselves`.
- `to` at the end → `too` (and it is unnecessary).
- "so that's the reason why" is spoken English, not academic.
- Comma splice again.

**Sentence 10:** "But some people said they just want to have more oppertunity to meet new people in the world."
- `oppertunity` → `opportunity` (spelling).
- Correct idea (meeting people), but no number (9%) and you did not say it was the **least** popular reason, which is the key point.
- Do not start a sentence with "But" in formal writing.

**Sentence 11:** "Cost is also a aspect that make adult thing a lot."
- "a aspect" → "an aspect" (a before a vowel sound becomes an).
- "make" → "makes" (subject-verb agreement), or better, rewrite.
- "make adult thing a lot": unclear. "thing" is probably "think" (typo). Even so, this is opinion, not data. Delete.

**Sentence 12:** "Most of them believe that costs should be shared between Taxpayer, Individual and Employer."
- "Most" is not supported: no single group had a majority.
- Capital letters are not needed.
- "between" is for two; use "among" three parties.
- Good that you included the three groups. Now give all three figures.

**Sentence 13:** "Individual should pay 40% and Employer should support employee 35% of cost, and Taxpayer should pay the rest."
- Good: correct figures for individual and employer.
- Articles missing: "Individuals", "Employers", "taxpayers", "the cost".
- "support employee 35%" → "contribute 35%".
- "the rest" → give the number: 25%.
- You stop abruptly: there is no summary or link back to the bar chart.
